The first 25 nodes

Help start one of the first 25 nodes.

A node is a group of people who help each other and make their own rules. Each one needs at least one maker, one supporter and one maintainer. Anyone, anywhere, can apply.

Seven short questions. At the end your email app opens with your answers, and you send them yourself. What each role does

Which role fits you?

In your own words, in any language. “I sell eggs at the market on Saturdays” is a good answer.

Who would be in your node?

For example: it’s hard to find a tailor here.

Where are you?

Not your address. The town is enough.

Do you know people for the other two roles?
Can we list you by name?

You can change this later.

Check your answers.

Nothing goes out until you press send in your email app.

Email app didn’t open? Copy your answers and send them from any email to research at clansurf dot com.

Who can apply, and what you get
Who can apply
Anyone, anywhere. You don’t need a qualification.
What we ask
Give the time you can, and agree with the idea behind Clansurf.
What you get
Your name, or one you choose, on the list of the first 25 nodes, and a public record of what you did.
How joining works
We reply to everyone. Existing members vote, and one maker, one supporter and one maintainer must say yes. Nodes that already have all three start first.

Your answers

  • They go to HuyoWorld, which is researching Clansurf, as an email you send yourself. This page stores nothing.
  • We use them only to plan the first nodes and to reply to you.
  • We pass your details to others only if you ask us to.
  • Write to research at clansurf dot com to see or delete them.
  • Your name goes on the site only if you say yes.
