Where people and local tech meet everyday human needs.

Clansurf helps a group of people make their node productive. People help each other and contribute in their own way, and the node’s own rules stop anyone from abusing it. It works with tech they own themselves, for everyday needs like food, clothing, repairs and computing.

What local means

Local means owned, not nearby.

A street where the neighbours run their own radios is local. So is someone in Norway who runs their own server, or their own AI, and uses it to help someone in Mauritius. They’re thousands of kilometres apart, but they own the tech. Nobody rents it from a platform that can change the rules or switch it off.

So there are two kinds of node: one tied to a place, and one tied to what people own.

How it works

Every node decides for itself.

A node is a group of people working together. It makes its own decisions: who joins, who leaves, its own rules and what it offers. Nobody outside can overrule them, Clansurf included.

  1. Makers

    The people who make or provide what others need. The egg seller, the tailor, the carpenter, the person running a server.

  2. Supporters

    The people who use what makers offer, share it with others and help keep the node going.

  3. Maintainers

    The people who make sure the node works and keeps to its principles. They help people join and get found, keep notes on what works and what breaks, and train the next maintainer.

    Joining a node takes a yes from one of each: a maker, a supporter and a maintainer. Then the node’s members vote. The people who approve you can’t be your relatives, and a maker can’t approve someone in their own trade.

The network body is small on purpose. It can’t overrule a node, and it doesn’t run one. The details are in the docs.

Use cases

The first one: a single street.

Most streets have someone selling eggs, someone who sews, someone who fixes things. The people fifty metres away often don’t know. A node on the street makes them findable, on radios the neighbours own, and it keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

Vision

Shaping humanity to serve, to be better and to be productive, for good reasons.

  1. People help each other.Neighbours find the people who can help them, without asking a platform first.
  2. What people do is recorded honestly.Everyone keeps the credit for what they contribute, and nobody can quietly change the record.
  3. Nobody can abuse it.Each node writes its own rules, and those rules stop any one person or group from taking over.

25+

25 or more nodes of different kinds, anywhere in the world.

Each one is built with the people who will run it. The first 25 are starting now. Help start one.

Local AI agents that work for the node, not the other way round.

Keeping a record current is real clerical work, and it falls on the maintainers. Agents running on the node’s own hardware can take that on. They never decide anything.
